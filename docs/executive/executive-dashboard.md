## What the board says, in one paragraph

R&J's **strategy and capital thinking are well ahead of its stage** — genuinely a strength, and rare. But the four dimensions that determine whether the strategy is *true* — Customer Understanding, Market Validation, Product Readiness, Operations — are all weak, and they are weak for **one connected reason**: the company has not yet gathered the evidence, or kept the core promise, that everything else assumes. The dashboard's shape (A‑ head, D legs) is itself the diagnosis. **Every point of strategy added while the legs stay at D lowers the company's honesty, not raises its value.**

---

## The through-line risk (why the grades cluster)

The dimensions are not independently weak — they share a root:

```
   Zero customer evidence  ─┐
                            ├─►  We don't know if the Certainty-Seeker is real,
   Core promise unkept     ─┤     feels agony (painkiller) or mild anxiety (vitamin),
   (stock room ≠ your room) │     or will pay a premium — yet Strategy, Capital,
                            │     Market, and Product decisions all assume she will.
   Refundable deposit      ─┘
   ≠ demand
```

Close the root and **four grades move at once** (Customer, Market Validation, Product, Capital). That is the definition of the highest-leverage action, and it is why the [Situation Assessment](situation-assessment.md) names a single sprint rather than eight department plans.

---

## Standing recommendations to the CEO

1. **Freeze the strategic superstructure.** Do not commission Phases 05–13 or the Marketing/Ops/Finance/Engineering brains until the core is validated. They will be better, cheaper, and honest once written on evidence. *(Trade-off: it feels like slowing down; in fact it prevents building six documents on a possibly-false premise.)*
2. **Run the "Prove the Core" sprint** ([Situation Assessment](situation-assessment.md)) as the company's sole priority for the next ~4–6 weeks.
3. **Answer the two founder unknowns that gate sequencing** — real runway and compliance status (Capital §9). Until then, urgency is unquantified.
4. **Update this dashboard when the sprint moves a grade** — the target is Customer D→C and Product C‑→C within one cycle.
