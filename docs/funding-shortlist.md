# R&J Interiors — Funding & Support Shortlist

> **Research date:** 2026-07-08
> **Method:** Deep multi-source research (103 agents, 21 sources fetched, 89 claims extracted, 24/25 adversarially verified via 3-vote).
> **Scope:** Global / remote-friendly orgs open to a Kenyan **pre-launch** startup — across grants & competitions, accelerators/incubators, and angel/pre-seed VC. Matched on four angles: creative-tech/proptech, women-led (Rose), creative-economy/craft, youth/local-SME.

## The one truth that shapes everything
**Stage-fit is the #1 filter.** R&J is pre-launch / pre-revenue with a working prototype. So **grants, competitions, and women-founder programs are the friendliest doors**; most equity funds prefer an MVP with early traction. Wherever we approach anyone, **lead with the working VR/AR visualizer + the Kickstarter deposit numbers as proof of validated demand.**

Two sharpest, under-used levers:
- **Rose Kabathi** = woman co-founder → unlocks women-founder-gated funds.
- **Simon Juma** = technical co-founder → required by some pre-seed funds.

1. **UNDP timbuktoo Creative Hub** (incubator) — the only one that explicitly welcomes *ideation-stage*.
2. **Standard Chartered Women in Tech** (accelerator + grant) — women-led + tech.

## 1) Grants & Competitions (non-dilutive — keep 100% ownership)

### ⭐ AWIEF Pitch n Grow 2026 — pan-African women-founder pitch competition
- **What they want:** Women-led ventures. Runs an **"Idea Track" for pre-venture businesses with a validated concept + early prototype** (rare — accepts genuinely pre-launch). Non-dilutive.
- **Fit:** Women-led (Rose) + prototype. **Strong.**
- **Apply:** Online; a 2026 cycle (~July 2026 deadline) was referenced — **verify current window**.
- **Confidence:** Surfaced in search (secondary source) — confirm on awief.org.

### IYBA-WE4A — Women Entrepreneurship for Africa
- **What they want:** Women entrepreneurs in Africa; business support + financing via partners.
- **Fit:** Women-led (Rose). Verify current intake.

### Creative-economy watch-list (values fit, not pre-launch cheques *right now*)
- **HEVA Fund** — https://www.hevafund.com/funding — East Africa creative-economy financier. The East Africa Creative Business Fund (~$20–50K) is **debt for operational/growth-stage** creatives; that window is COVID-era ("active in 2022"), no confirmed open call. Still active (Dec 2025 NCBA partnership) — **monitor for new windows.**
- **Mastercard Foundation — Kenya Inclusive Creatives Program** — https://mastercardfdn.org/en/what-we-do/our-programs/kenya-inclusive-creatives-program/ — backs **youth-led & women-led** digital/creative enterprises, but via *implementing partners* as ecosystem/skills support, not direct startup cheques. **Find the delivery partners** for an entry point.

## 2) Accelerators / Incubators (mentorship + small cheque + network)

### ⭐⭐ UNDP timbuktoo Creative Hub — virtual creative-tech incubation — BEST pure pre-launch fit
- **Link:** https://www.undp.org/south-africa/press-releases/calling-african-creative-entrepreneurs-apply-creative-tech-incubation-programme
- **What they want:** Founders **"at the ideation or early stage"** (pre-launch welcome); **encourages 18–35 & women-led**; creative/design/multimedia. Fully virtual, ~20–30 selected. **Non-dilutive.**
- **Fit:** Hits creative-tech + women-led (Rose) + young founders — **strongest match found.**
- **Catch:** 2025 cohort closed (applied June 2025). **Watch for next intake.** (UNDP page sometimes 403s; corroborated via partner UVU Africa.)

### ⭐⭐ Standard Chartered Women in Tech Accelerator (with Village Capital) — BEST women-led fit
- **Links:** https://vilcap.com/programs/women-in-tech-accelerator-2026 · https://womenintech.ke
- **What they want:** **A woman leading an impact-creating, tech-enabled startup.** Kenya eligible; **non-dilutive grants** (Kenya cohort up to ~$10K seed).
- **Fit:** Rose + VR visualizer = women-led + tech-enabled. **Strong.**
- **Catch:** Emphasizes "impact." Frame R&J's impact as **women's economic empowerment, craft/manufacturing jobs, SME digitization, Nyeri (non-Nairobi)** — not "premium curtains."

### The Baobab Network — pan-African, remote-first accelerator
- **Link:** https://thebaobabnetwork.com/
- **What they want:** **$100K for 12.5% equity**; 12-week intensive on product-market fit, traction, fundraising. Remote — no relocation.
- **Catch:** Prefers **MVP + traction** — R&J is at the early edge. Lead with prototype + deposits.

### Accelerate Africa (by Future Africa) — elite pan-African accelerator
- **Link:** https://acceler8.africa/
- **What they want:** **Earliest-stage** African founders solving big problems; **takes no equity/funding to join** (optional $250K–500K pre-seed after).
- **Catch:** Brutally competitive (~1,900 applications → 10 selected).

### 🔒 Not eligible yet — revisit once there's revenue
- **Google for Startups Accelerator: Africa** — https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/africa/ — equity-free but **needs growth-stage + revenue/traction**.
- **Safaricom Spark** — https://www.safaricom.co.ke/sparkaccelerator/ — **requires existing product AND revenue** (great *future* target given M-Pesa alignment).
- **Y Combinator** — https://www.ycombinator.com/apply — accepts African founders but **requires relocating to San Francisco** (fails remote requirement).

## 3) Angel Networks / Pre-Seed VC (equity cheques)

### ⭐⭐ FirstCheck Africa — pre-seed VC for female-founded startups — TOP equity fit
- **Link:** https://www.firstcheck.africa/
- **What they want:** **At least one female founder/co-founder** (Rose qualifies). Defines pre-seed as **"idea to launched product"** — accepts pre-launch. **$25K–$250K** for equity. Invests in Kenya (Tushop, Jumba, Uncover, Akiba Digital).
- **Catch:** In practice leans data/traction-oriented and Nigeria-heavy. *(A claim that they hard-require traction was refuted in verification — traction is a preference, not a gate.)*

### ⭐ Microtraction — pre-seed, $100K, technical-founder fund
- **Link:** https://www.microtraction.com/
- **What they want:** **At least one technical co-founder** (Simon qualifies); "earliest stage"; active in Kenya. **$100K** for equity.
- **Catch:** Stated criteria expect an MVP + validated demand → **lead with the working visualizer + deposits.**

### ⭐ Madica (by Flourish Ventures) — structured 18-month pre-seed program
- **Links:** https://madica.vc/program · https://madica.vc/faqs
- **What they want:** African teams, full-time founders, **no relocation**; **explicitly prioritizes women-led & non-hub founders** (>50% female-led portfolio). **Up to $200K.** MVP preferred **but** founders without one qualify via **market validation (waitlist, sign-ups, offline/pre-sales)** — deposits fit this.
- **Catch:** Classifies **Kenya as an established hub**, so the "non-hub" edge is weak at country level — lean on **women-led (Rose)** + cite **Nyeri**.

### Ajim Capital — pre-seed/seed VC, proptech vertical
- **Link:** https://ajimcapital.com/
- **What they want:** Sub-Saharan Africa; **$250K–$500K**; lists **proptech** among target verticals (creative-tech/proptech angle).
- **Confidence:** Surfaced in fetch — verify current thesis/stage.

### Angel networks — how to reach individual angels
- **ABAN (African Business Angel Network)** — umbrella of **~77 angel networks across 37 countries**; gateway to early cheques.
- **InVhestia / ViKtoria Ventures (Nairobi)** — helps early-stage Kenyan companies raise from **local angels** and build networks — practical local route.

## How to play it
- **Now (non-dilutive, pre-launch-friendly):** timbuktoo (next intake), Women in Tech, AWIEF Idea Track.
- **Now (equity, using our levers):** FirstCheck (Rose) + Microtraction (Simon) + Madica (women/validation).
- **Keep warm for later (need revenue):** Safaricom Spark, Google for Startups, Baobab.

## Caveats before applying
1. **Verify current deadlines** — several run cohort windows that may be closed even where we're eligible (timbuktoo, Women in Tech, Baobab, AWIEF).
2. **Kickstarter deposits = our traction story** — quantify them (X backers, KSh Y committed pre-launch) in every application; that's what bridges the "pre-revenue" gap for equity funds.
3. **One refuted claim (for accuracy):** the idea that FirstCheck Africa *hard-requires* existing traction was NOT upheld in verification (1–2 vote) — treat traction there as a preference.
