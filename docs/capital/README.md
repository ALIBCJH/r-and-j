# R&J Interiors — Capital Brain

**The Capital Operating System.** Version 1.0 · Established 2026-07-09 · Owner: Founders (Rose Kabathi, Simon Juma)
Standing team: Capital Strategy (VC partner · angel · grant writer · startup CFO · investment banker · fundraising strategist · startup lawyer · IR manager · financial analyst · ecosystem researcher)

---

> **What the Capital Brain is.** A permanent system for ensuring R&J always has access to the financial, strategic, and partnership resources it needs to pursue its mission. Its objective is **not to find funding** — it is to **maximise the company's access to capital across its whole life**, at the lowest cost in dilution, effort, and time.
>
> **What it is not.** It is not a list of grants. A list optimises for quantity; this system optimises for **expected return**. Every opportunity is scored, ranked, and either scheduled with a next action or explicitly parked with a reason.

---

## The one idea that governs everything

**Capital is not just money, and money is not all equal.** A KSh 1M grant, a KSh 1M loan, and KSh 1M of equity have wildly different true costs. The Capital Brain treats every source through one lens — **Expected Capital Value (ECV)** — that nets the cash on offer against the probability of getting it and the price you pay in ownership, effort, and time. See [`02-scoring-model.md`](02-scoring-model.md).

**Capital, in full, includes:** grants · equity · debt · accelerators · incubators · corporate partnerships · government support · creative-economy funds · innovation challenges · equipment financing · strategic partnerships · customer financing · revenue. The Capital Brain works all thirteen — see the ranked, sequenced stack in [`01-capital-stack.md`](01-capital-stack.md).

## The non-negotiables of this system

1. **Optimise for expected return, never quantity.** A ranked shortlist of 6 well-fit doors beats 60 long-shots.
2. **Non-dilutive first.** At R&J's stage, equity is the most expensive money on the table (see [`00-capital-strategy.md §3`](00-capital-strategy.md)). Exhaust grants, competitions, credits, and the right debt before selling ownership.
3. **Every opportunity carries the full 12-field schema** (Executive Summary · Amount · Equity Required · Eligibility · Deadline · Strategic Fit · Probability · Preparation · Documents · Estimated ROI · Recommended Timing · Next Action). No half-entries.
4. **Truth over politeness about readiness.** The register is honest about what would sink an application ([`04-readiness-gaps.md`](04-readiness-gaps.md)). Capital chases companies that are *fundable*, and this system's first job is to make R&J fundable.
5. **The story is one story.** Every application tells the same true narrative: a trust company (not a tech costume), women- and technically-co-founded, with a working visualizer and a validated-demand instrument — climbing from a service margin to a platform. Consistency is a fundraising asset; see [`assets/narrative-and-pitch-outline.md`](assets/narrative-and-pitch-outline.md).
6. **Capital serves the mission, not vanity.** We do not raise to look raised. We raise the *right* capital, at the *right* time, for the *right* milestone. Mission beats a headline number (Company Brain Principle 8).

## How to operate the Capital Brain (the monthly loop)

1. **Refresh the register** — verify open windows/deadlines (many are cohort-gated and go stale fast), add newly-surfaced opportunities, re-score.
2. **Re-rank by ECV** and confirm the top 3 "act now" targets.
3. **Advance the CRM** — move live conversations along stages; log every contact; set the next action + date.
4. **Close the readiness gaps** that are blocking the top targets (data, documents, product fixes).
5. **Report** — one page: pipeline value, probability-weighted expected capital, what moved, what's blocked, next month's focus.

## Provenance & links to the Company Brain

The Capital Brain is downstream of the strategic truth established in the **Company Brain** (`docs/phase-01…04`). It inherits, and must stay consistent with:
- **Identity** — R&J is a **trust/certainty company, not a tech company** (Phase 01; Strategy Review). Fundraising that oversells "tech" invites the "4.5/10 — good business in a tech costume" verdict. We raise on the *real* moat: vertical integration + local craft trust + designer-engineer team.
- **Scale path** — H1 curtains are **not venture-scale** (capacity-bound, single-digit-millions KSh/yr; Phase 03). The venture case is trust-transfers-to-adjacencies: H2 own-the-room → H3 B2B/platform. **Match the capital type to the horizon** (grants/debt fund H1; equity funds the H2/H3 ambition).
- **Evidence honesty** — unit economics are grade-C assumptions and customer validation is grade-hypothesis (Phases 02, 04). The Capital Brain does not launder assumptions into "traction." It builds the instruments that turn them into evidence ([`04-readiness-gaps.md`](04-readiness-gaps.md)).
- **The existing shortlist** (`docs/funding-shortlist.md`, 2026-07-08) is the seed research for grants/accelerators/VC; it is absorbed and extended here into the full register.
