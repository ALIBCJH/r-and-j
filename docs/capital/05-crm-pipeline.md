# 05 — Capital CRM & Pipeline

**R&J Interiors · Capital Brain**
Status: Live working document · Version 1.0 · 2026-07-09
Owner: IR Manager (founder-held) · Review cadence: **Weekly** (this is the operational heartbeat)

> **What this is.** The investor-quality CRM: every capital relationship and application, what stage it's at, what happens next, and by when. The [register](03-opportunity-register.md) decides *what to pursue*; the CRM tracks *the live pursuit*. **Nothing is "in progress" without a next action and a date.**

---

## 1. Pipeline stages

| Stage | Definition | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **0 · Identified** | In the register, scored, not yet started | Decide to pursue → Stage 1 |
| **1 · Preparing** | Assembling the application/materials for this door | Materials ready → submit |
| **2 · Submitted / Applied** | Application in; awaiting response | Response received |
| **3 · In Conversation** | Live dialogue (call, diligence, interview round) | Decision or term sheet |
| **4 · Committed / Awarded** | Yes received; finalising terms/docs | Money in hand |
| **5 · Closed-Won** | Capital received | — (report + relationship-manage) |
| **✗ · Closed-Lost / Parked** | No, or parked for a milestone/window | Log reason + revisit date |

**WIP limit:** keep **3–5 opportunities in Stages 1–3** at once ([`00-capital-strategy.md §7`](00-capital-strategy.md)). More than that means founder attention is spread too thin.

## 4. The weekly loop (15 minutes)

1. **Advance every Stage 1–3 row** — did the next action happen? Set the new one + date.
2. **Chase overdue next-actions** — anything past its date gets done or explicitly re-dated.
3. **Respect the WIP limit** — if >5 active, pause the lowest-ECV one.
4. **Log every touch** in §3.
5. **Pull the next opportunity** from the register into Stage 1 when a slot frees up.

## 5. The monthly report (one page — see [`README.md`](README.md) monthly loop)

- **Pipeline value:** Σ target amounts in Stages 1–4.
- **Probability-weighted expected capital:** Σ (amount × P(success)) — the honest expected haul.
- **Moved this month:** stage changes, wins, losses.
- **Blocked:** what's stuck, on which readiness gap ([`04`](04-readiness-gaps.md)).
- **Next month's focus:** the 3 top-ECV actions.

---

## 6. Relationship management (Stage 5 and beyond)

Winning capital is the start of a relationship, not the end. For every closed-won source:
- **Report on time**, even when not required — funders talk to each other; on-time reporting is future capital.
- **Send unsolicited good news** (a milestone, a great order) — keeps warm sources warm for follow-on.
- **Ask for the next intro** — a happy funder is the best source of the next one.
- **Never surprise a funder with bad news** — flag problems early; it's the trust thesis applied to investors.
