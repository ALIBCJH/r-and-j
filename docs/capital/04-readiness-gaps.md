## 2. The fix plan (sequenced)

### Wave 0 — Unblock (days; do immediately) — closes **P0** gaps
These are cheap, fast, and unblock whole tiers of capital or protect the core thesis:
- **G4 / Compliance:** confirm business registration; obtain/verify **KRA PIN** and tax compliance; register with **MSEA via eCitizen** (low effort, unlocks government funds, tenders, Buy-Kenya procurement). *Owner: Founders.*
- **G6 / Price bug:** reconcile homepage `TextilesShowcase` prices with catalog `products.ts` — a ~2× contradiction on the *same products* directly corrodes the trust the company sells. Fix before any paid traffic or investor demo. *Owner: Product.*
- **G9 / Runway:** founders state real months-of-runway at current burn. This is the first input to the capital *sequence* ([`00-capital-strategy.md §9`](00-capital-strategy.md)). *Owner: Founders.*

### Wave 1 — Make it fundable (this quarter) — closes **P1** gaps
The high-leverage work that turns "nice idea" into "diligence-ready":
- **G1 / Customer validation:** run **15–20 structured customer interviews** (Phase 02 §12 has the plan). Move personas & willingness-to-pay from hypothesis to evidence. Cheapest, highest-leverage unlock in the whole document. *Owner: Rose.*
- **G2 / Intent instrument:** convert the refundable founding deposit into something that **measures committed intent** — e.g. a non-refundable (or credit-only) reservation, a signed order with a real deposit, or a waitlist with a concrete next step. Then you can *honestly* cite it as traction. *Owner: Product/GTM.*
- **G3 / Unit economics:** **export order data from KV** and compute true **gross margin per order** (not per panel) after fabric, making-labour, consultation credit, delivery, install (Phase 04 §11). This single number gates debt underwriting and survives equity diligence. *Owner: Simon/Finance.*
- **G5 / Data room:** assemble data-room v1 (see [`assets/data-room-checklist.md`](assets/data-room-checklist.md)) — reuse across every application. *Owner: Founders.*
- **G8 / VR claim:** correct copy so the narrative claims only what ships (the web Studio), framing Meta Quest VR explicitly as roadmap/vision. Protects the honesty of the tech story. *Owner: Product/Copy.*
- **G10 / Identity sentence:** pick the one-sentence identity (the Company Brain resolves this: **a trust/certainty company** whose wedge is the window). Put it at the top of [`assets/narrative-and-pitch-outline.md`](assets/narrative-and-pitch-outline.md) and use it everywhere. *Owner: Founders.*

### Wave 2 — Sharpen (next quarter) — closes **P2**
- **G7 / "Actual room" product truth:** either ship real compositing of curtains onto the user's uploaded room photo, **or** adjust the promise to match what the product does (re-tinting stock scenes). Do not demo a capability that isn't there. *Owner: Product (Phase 06 brief).*

### Wave 3 — Time unlocks it — **P3**
- **G11 / Trading history:** accrues automatically with launch + consistent M-Pesa sales. At ~6 months it unlocks Pezesha/4G/bank SME debt; at ~2 years, KIE machinery finance. **Nothing to "fix" — just launch, trade, and keep clean M-Pesa records** (that transaction record *is* the key to Tier-2 debt).
