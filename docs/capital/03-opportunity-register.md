- **Eligibility:** Early-stage; self-serve tiers need only a registered company + a working product website — **pre-launch qualifies.** The big tiers ($150k+ at some) that require VC/accelerator affiliation are out of reach until R&J joins a qualifying accelerator — *that affiliation is itself a high-value unlock.*

- **Strategic fit:** **Strong** — subsidises the visualizer; "creative-tech" credibility. *(Multiplier ~1.3.)*
