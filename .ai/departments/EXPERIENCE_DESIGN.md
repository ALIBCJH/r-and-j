# Experience Design — Department Charter

> R&J Interiors · Operating OS (`.ai/departments`) · **This file is the model charter.**
> Owner: **Rose** (aesthetic & taste) · **Simon** (the tool) · Source: [Phase 06 Product](../../docs/phase-06-product.md), [Phase 05 Brand](../../docs/phase-05-brand.md) · Status: v1 · 2026-07-09

## 1. Mandate
**Manufacture certainty.** Design every customer-facing experience — the Studio, the picker, the consultation, the install handover — so the customer *sees the result honestly and trusts it before they commit.* The experience is not decoration; it is the delivery mechanism for the company's one promise: **"you will not be surprised."**

## 2. Owns / Doesn't own
- **Owns:** the Studio & style-picker UX; the certainty engine's *experience* (how recommendations are shown, how honest the preview feels); the consultation and install-handover experience; the *feel* of the funnel (a trust escalator, not a pressure funnel — Phase 09 §2).
- **Does NOT own:** the engine's *code* (→ Engineering); the *fabric/product* design (→ Operations/craft, Rose's atelier hat); paid acquisition or channel strategy (→ Growth); brand verbal/visual *identity system* (→ Brand — Experience Design *applies* it, doesn't define it).
- **Boundary rule:** Experience Design decides *what the experience should feel like and do*; Engineering decides *how it's built*; Brand decides *what it looks and sounds like as a system*.

## 3. Governing principles (operationalised)
- **Honesty over spectacle** (Phase 01 §10.3, Belief 4). A preview may never look better than the delivered curtain. When unsure, **show less, not a prettier lie** — the `colorEngine` "refuse to fake a match" rule is the template (Phase 06 §4).
- **Every screen names the uncertainty it removes** (Phase 01 §10.2). No experience ships to *impress*; it ships to make the customer *more certain*.
- **Solve it by hand first** (Principle 5). Encode Rose's real consultation judgment; don't invent an interaction she wouldn't perform in person.
- **The human is the trust layer** (Belief 7). The tool builds confidence *toward* the consultation, never replaces it.

## 4. Decision rights
- **Two-way door (owner decides fast):** copy tweaks, layout, interaction details, which recommendation to surface first, micro-flows.
- **One-way door (joint, in writing):** the *core promise* the experience makes ("your room" vs "an example room" — Phase 06 §5), any claim about capability, removing/adding a funnel gate, anything touching the honesty law.
- **Rose has final say** on aesthetic/taste and the consultation experience; **Simon** on what the tool can honestly do. Conflicts resolve to: *does this make the customer more certain and is it true?* If yes to both, ship.

## 5. Standards & Definition of Done
An experience is **done** when:
1. It removes a **named** customer uncertainty (write it down).
2. Every claim it makes is **true today** (honesty law) — no unshipped capability implied in copy, image, or alt-text.
3. It captures the **signal** it should (Phase 07 §3 event) — a screen that teaches R&J nothing is unfinished.
4. It funnels toward **trust** (the consultation / Promise-Kept), not toward a vanity action.
5. It works on a **phone, on Kenyan mobile data** (the real device — Phase 08 mobile-first Studio).

## 6. Current priorities (M0 → M1)
1. **Own the honesty pass on all experience surfaces** (M0, keystone): retire the VR-headset hero + "actual room"/"step into your finished home" claims; align to the shipped Studio (Phase 06 step 1, Phase 11 R1). *Experience Design is the accountable department for this.*
2. **Wire photo → wall-sample → recommendation** (M1): turn the two disconnected half-studios into one honest "fabrics matched to *your* wall" experience (Phase 06 §5). Feasible now — the primitives exist.
3. **Design the install-handover moment** as the peak-delight experience that triggers referral (Phase 09 §4) — the experience doesn't end at checkout.

## 7. Metrics
- **Preview-vs-Delivered match** (post-install) — the experience's truest score; the Promise-Kept signal (Phase 01 §12, Phase 06 §8).
- **Recommendation acceptance** — do customers pick a suggested fabric? (Engine + experience validation.)
- **Preview → consultation conversion** — does the experience build enough trust to book?
- **Anti-metric (rejected):** raw Studio sessions/plays (Phase 01 §12, Phase 02 §9 "Tech-Toy Tourist"). Engagement that doesn't convert to trust is noise.

## 8. Interfaces
- **← Customer Insight:** feeds the real blocker (does the customer need *her* room, or is a matched recommendation enough? — Phase 06 §0.3). Experience Design does not guess this; it waits for M2 evidence before the expensive compositing build.
- **← Brand:** supplies the identity system (palette, type, voice — Phase 05); Experience Design applies it.
- **→ Engineering:** hands over *what to build and why*; receives *what's feasible* (e.g. the compositing spike — Phase 06 §6).
- **→ Growth:** the experience *is* the funnel Growth drives traffic into; both must agree the funnel is honest before any spend (sequencing law).
- **→ Operations:** the install-handover experience is co-owned with the install SOP (Phase 08 §3).

## 9. Anti-goals
- **No VR/AR/AI spectacle for its own sake** (Phase 06 §7). No experience that impresses while over-promising.
- **No generative "reimagine your room" theatre** that risks a beautiful lie (Belief 4).
- **No dark patterns / manufactured urgency** (Phase 09 §6). The trust escalator never manipulates.
- **No experience that captures no signal** (Phase 07 §3) or that a founder can't stand behind honestly.
